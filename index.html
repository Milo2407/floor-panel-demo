Wall panel for Home Assistant · live demo

Your floor plan, live on the wall.

Floor Panel is a lightweight Home Assistant wall panel built around your own floor plan. Rooms are coloured by temperature, lights and heating glow where they're on, open windows swing open, and a tap on a room opens its controls. Try it below with a made-up house.

It's live: tap a room for its controls, switch floors, try All off and Bedtime. Everything here is made-up data for a fictional two-storey house; this page doesn't connect to any Home Assistant.

Built for slow wall screens

One small page of about 80 KB instead of a full dashboard, so old tablets and wall displays show your home straight away.

Your rooms, not a template

Every room, door, window and staircase comes from your plan, across as many floors as you have.

No add-ons on the screen

Copy a folder into Home Assistant's www folder, sign in once, done. No custom cards, no YAML, no CDN.

Private by design

It uses Home Assistant's normal login; no token or password is stored in any file. Bathrooms always stay plain and can't be tapped.

Draw your own home in the browser

The starter kit includes a plan editor. Open a photo or scan of your floor plan, click the corners of each room, name it, mark doors and windows, and save. Lines snap straight and to nearby corners.

  • Your plan picture never leaves your browser
  • Several floors with a floor switcher on the panel
  • A setup view that numbers every window for your sensors
  • Rooms point at your own sensors, lights and radiators in one config file
